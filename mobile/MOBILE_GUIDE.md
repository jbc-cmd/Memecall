# 📱 MemeCall on Mobile (Android & iPhone / iPad)

You can run MemeCall directly on mobile phones in Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams!

---

## 🌟 Method 1: Mobile Chromium Browsers (Kiwi Browser / Orion Browser / Lemur)
*Best experience with full extension features!*

1. Download **Kiwi Browser** or **Lemur Browser** (Android) or **Orion Browser** (iOS).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Kiwi/Orion.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** and tap **Load unpacked** (or zip/folder upload).
4. Select the `Memecall` extension folder.
5. Open [meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com) or [zoom.us](https://zoom.us).
6. MemeCall's touch-draggable floating pill (🎭) will automatically appear in your call!

---

## 🌟 Method 2: Userscript (Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey / Stay app / Userscripts on Safari)
*Works on Safari iOS & Chrome/Brave/Firefox Android!*

1. Install a userscript manager:
   - **iOS Safari**: Install the free **Userscripts** or **Stay** extension from the iOS App Store.
   - **Android**: Install **Violentmonkey** / **Tampermonkey** on Firefox or Kiwi.
2. Add [`memecall.user.js`](./memecall.user.js) into your userscript manager.
3. Join any Google Meet or Zoom call in your mobile browser.
4. Strike poses or tap the floating **🎭 MemeCall** touch pill to fire memes live from your phone!
